Published science beneath the meme

All three brains use a leaky integrate-and-fire model of the kind published by Shiu et al. in Nature.

Each neuron accumulates electrical input. Synaptic strength follows the number of contacts observed in the electron-microscope reconstruction. Predicted neurotransmitters determine whether a connection excites or inhibits. When a neuron crosses its threshold, it fires.

There is no training process teaching the brains how to behave. No language model interprets the chart. No optimizer searches for profitable decisions.

And there is no separate "cuck model." The third network is not a different architecture, a mood system, or a character with a personality file. It is the same wiring as the other two.

The only difference is what is plugged into it.

Sensation in, at full gain.

Motor commands out, into nothing.

That single change is the entire project.

The third brain

In a real fly, descending neurons carry commands from the brain toward the body. Walk forward. Turn. Back away. Groom. Freeze. Extend the proboscis. Escape.

The cuck has all of them. They fire constantly. The panel shows their rates in hertz.

They are not connected to anything.

This is the honest center of the project and it is also the joke: those are real commands, measured where they stop. The bar marked "stand up → nothing" is a live readout of a motor intention with no downstream path. When it saturates, it turns red. Nothing else happens, because nothing else is wired.

The model has no spontaneous activity, so a brain with nothing to sense is silent. The cuck is never silent. He is the most consistently stimulated network on the site and the only one whose output is discarded.

He also blacks out more often than the couple, and the counter is public. That is not a gag written into the copy. It is what an input-only network does when the model has no fatigue: activity accumulates, runs away, and the brain restarts.

The brains drift apart

All three simulations include limited Hebbian plasticity: synaptic strengths change slightly through repeated activity before relaxing slowly toward their original values.

The three begin with the same connectome. They do not remain in the same state.

They occupy different roles. They receive different stimuli. They generate different activity. They accumulate different internal histories.

The couple's histories are written by an act. His is written by watching one, at full sensory gain, with his mushroom bodies — the learning center — running the entire time.

Over a long enough run, the same market event enters three networks that are no longer in precisely the same state, and the one that has changed most is the one that did the least.


What is real — and what is designed

The FlyWire connectome, the leaky integrate-and-fire model and NeuroMechFly are genuine scientific foundations. The anatomy on screen is real anatomy: region positions, region proportions, and the neuropils named in the legend.

What runs in your browser is that model at display scale, and the footer says so — it reports how many units are being drawn and that they are scaled from 3 x 138,639. The site renders a representative population per brain and drives it with the same integrate-and-fire dynamics, rather than streaming a 150-million-synapse simulation into your tab. Everything on the panel is measured from the model that is actually running. None of it is a script.

But the accurate sentence is "a whole-brain model at display scale," not "the whole connectome in a browser tab," and the site should never be read as claiming the second one.

The sensory translation is creative engineering. A buy is not biologically sugar. A sell is not inherently bitter. The libido system, the thresholds, and the mapping between market events and identified neural populations were designed.

So was the cuck's gain map. Flies do not have cope. They do not have humiliation, and they do not have hope in the sense the panel uses the word. Those are labels placed on channels whose behavior is defined in the open.

What is not designed is the structure underneath the labels. A network wired for sensory input and disconnected from its own motor output really does behave the way the panel shows: it saturates, it accumulates, it runs away, and it has no way to discharge.

That part is not a metaphor. The metaphor is just sitting on top of it, in a chair.

You are not watching an animal that understands crypto, or sex, or exclusion in a human sense. You are watching three constrained nervous systems in an artificial environment, reacting through the wiring available to them — and one of them has less wiring available than the others.
